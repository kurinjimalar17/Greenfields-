Useful Farming Tips

Good farming practices help farmers grow healthy crops, save resources and improve soil quality.

1. Take Care of the Soil

Use compost and organic matter to improve soil fertility. Test the soil before choosing crops.

2. Use Water Wisely

Water plants according to their needs. Drip irrigation can help reduce water wastage.

3. Choose Good Seeds

Select healthy seeds that are suitable for the local climate and growing season.

4. Practice Crop Rotation

Growing different crops in rotation can help maintain soil nutrients and reduce pest problems.

5. Control Pests Carefully

Check plants regularly and use suitable, safe pest-control methods when needed.
